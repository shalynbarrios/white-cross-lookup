Set up your cross below and get the shortest fix, move by move. When you want to learn the patterns, the step-by-step guide starts from a solved cross and works up to fixing two bad edges.

Hold the cube with white on the bottom and green facing you. Scrambles are given the standard way — white up, green front — then you flip the cube upside down and solve. In the practice cases the missing piece is white-green, and its home is bottom-front.

Step 1 · The solved cross, and knowing your colors

This is the goal: all four white edges on the bottom, each one's side sticker matching the center it touches. Before fixing anything, make sure you know which color sits beside which.

Quick flashcards

White stays on the bottom. If you know each color's neighbours, a misplaced edge tells you instantly which way to send it.

Step 2 · One edge is wrong

Three edges are home, one needs help. Set each case up on a real cube with its scramble, then play the fix move by move.

Step 3 · Two edges are wrong

Now two pieces need help at once. The same ideas apply — the solver just orders the moves so one fix doesn't undo the other.

Pick a piece, then tap the square where its white sticker is. Pieces you don't move stay home, so you can look up one, two, three or all four at a time.
