Set up your cross below and get the shortest fix, move by move. When you want to learn the patterns, the one-edge cases cover all 17 places a single piece can hide.

Hold the cube with white on the bottom, then turn the whole cube so the empty slot is facing you. Every case is drawn that way: the missing piece is white-red, and its home is bottom-front.
